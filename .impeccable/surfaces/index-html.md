# Surface brief: index.html (landing de inicio)

Mode: Persuade. Audience: academic jury (primary), patients. Job: understand in seconds that slots are real and that every appointment moves through stamped states; act by picking a free slot. Constraints: only name + cross logo are kept from the old look; no invented clinic facts; catalog names are placeholders from the DB; "Sobre la clínica" removed until real copy exists. Other pages keep the old world for now.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The landing is the clinic's carné de citas: every slot is a printed box, every state change a rubber-stamp impression. Refuses the category default of headline + doctor photo + row of service cards.

OWN-WORLD: Celeste cardstock (#BFD6EA) flooding whole regions, paler card face (#E6F0F8), print ink #14181F, stamp inks violet #5B3FA0 (pendiente), green #1F6B45 (confirmada), blue #1E4E8C (atendida), red #B42E28 (cancelada, logo). Archivo across widths for printed form labels and headlines, Public Sans for reading, Courier Prime for typed-in values and hours. Hairline ink rules, numbered fields 01–05, perforation lines, hatched struck slots.

STORY: The visitor sees a working carné, picks specialty → doctor → day → free hour, watches PENDIENTE land, and understands who stamps each later state (recepción, médico) and the rules behind it. Then reserves, or enters with their role.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Printed header strip with logo and tab-like nav. Left ~40%: headline "Tu cita, sellada y sin filas." at ~88px condensed caps, one-line sub, secondary link. Right ~60%: the carné itself at full scale with live fields 01 Especialidad, 02 Médico, 03 Día (next attending days), 04 Hora (real grid: free boxes clickable, taken/past hatched), 05 Estado with the stamp slot; primary action "Reservar este horario" inside the card.

FORM: Carné de citas, position 4 of the grounded list. seed:af32d57686981bc8. Signature interaction: choosing an hour drops the PENDIENTE stamp in one impression (no fade). Raises: cardstock floods regions (bebop); fixed numbered field order (six-pack); dense two-column rules on the card's reverse (Posada); single irreversible impression motion (ebru).

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
